### Highlights of Implemented Sections

1. **Hero & Dual Input Engine (`FreeScorePage.tsx`)**:
   - Split layout with drag-and-drop file upload (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`) and direct text paste.
   - Quick sample loaders (Software Developer, Marketing Lead, Sales Executive) for 1-click test runs.
   - Privacy guarantee badge ("We never share your data with 3rd parties or use it for AI model training").

2. **The 27 Crucial Checks Across 7 Categories**:
   - **ATS Essentials** (File format & size, ATS design, Professional email, Header links compliance, File naming, Dates & links consistency).
   - **Resume Sections** (Essential sections, Contact information, Section order hierarchy).
   - **Content** (ATS parse rate, Quantifying impact with metrics, Repetition of words & phrases, Spelling & passive duty grammar, Bullet length & density).
   - **Job Tailoring** (Hard skills match, Soft skills match, Action verbs velocity, Tailored job title).
   - **Recruiter Red Flags** (Resume credibility, Interview risk signals, Peer benchmarking, LinkedIn profile match).
   - **Bias & Discrimination** (Age & date bias prevention, Employment gaps transparency).
   - **Seniority & Impact** (Career progression & scope, Skills evidence in context, Leadership & ownership signals).

3. **Two-Tier Scoring Architecture**:
   - **Tier 1 (Machine ATS Parsability)**: Validates Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and Taleo reverse-engineered signals.
   - **Tier 2 (Recruiter Impact & Calibration)**: Evaluates quantifiable results (e.g. 30% increase) and Google XYZ formula density.

4. **Interactive Feature Showcase ("Put your resume score to work")**:
   - **Resume Builder**: Direct bridge to the Live CV Editor with vector PDF export.
   - **Cover Letter**: Targeted matching letters.
   - **Import Old CV**: Word / PDF / LinkedIn profile parser.
   - **Job Tracker**: Multi-application management.
   - **Interview Prep**: STAR stories and behavioral interview question generator.
   - **AI Job Matcher**: Real-time keyword gap analysis.

5. **Recruiter Intelligence & Myth-Busting Research**:
   - 6 comprehensive research cards covering common ATS myths, keyword strategies, and recruiter expectations in 2026.

6. **12-Item Comprehensive FAQ Accordion**:
   - Collapsible question/answer modules addressing every nuance of ATS compliance and scoring.

7. **Social Proof & Verified Reviews**:
   - 4.5/5 rating across 5,363+ candidates with authentic testimonials.
